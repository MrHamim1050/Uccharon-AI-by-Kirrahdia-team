## 4. Selling & sharing business — beginner crash course

**Ways to make money from a product:**
1. **Subscription (SaaS)** — users pay monthly. Best for apps like yours. Predictable revenue.
2. **Freemium** — free basic + paid premium. Good for growth, low conversion (2–5%).
3. **B2B licensing** — sell to schools/companies per-seat. Bigger cheques, longer sales.
4. **API / white-label** — other apps pay to use your pronunciation engine.
5. **Ads** — only works at 100k+ daily users. Skip for now.

**Ways to share ownership:**
1. **Equity (shares)** — investor owns % of company forever, gets % of future sale. Shark Tank = this.
2. **Revenue share** — investor gets X% of monthly revenue until they get 2–3× their money back. No ownership. Founder-friendly.
3. **Convertible note / SAFE** — investor gives cash now, converts to equity later at a discount when you raise a bigger round. Standard for early startups.
4. **Licensing** — someone pays you a fee to use your tech, you keep 100% ownership.

**Rules a first-time founder must remember:**
- Never sell equity you can't get back. It's permanent.
- Always keep **founders combined > 50%** after any deal.
- Reserve **10% ESOP** for future employees before raising.
- Get a written **term sheet** and a lawyer before signing. Verbal Shark Tank promises fail often.
- Valuation is a **negotiation**, not a fact. Justify with users, growth rate, market size (TAM).
